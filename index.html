A place to learn about Ka'a'awa!

Ka'a'awa is one of the most beautiful places on the Island of O'ahu, it is a small town located on the North Shore.

In Hawaiian, Ka'a'awa means "the wrasse". specifically the yellow wrasse fish, ʻaʻawa.

Fun facts about Ka'a'awa!

  • Ka'a'awa Valley has been used to film movies an TV shows such as Jurassic Park, Jumanji, King Kong: Skull Island, and Lost.
  • In ancient Hawaiʻi, Ka'a'awa's reef was renowned for its abundance of fish, which were prized by local fishermen.
  • Ka'a'awa's biggest store is a local 7/11
